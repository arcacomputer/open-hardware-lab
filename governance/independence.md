# Research independence policy

## Core rule

Funding, hardware access and attribution cannot purchase a favorable technical conclusion.

## Required disclosure

Every hardware unit record must disclose:

- exact manufacturer, model and tested configuration;
- whether the unit was purchased, loaned or permanently supplied;
- the retailer, provider or funding source;
- any cash payment, discount, travel support or accessory supplied;
- who owns the unit during and after testing;
- the intake date, custody period and return obligation when known.

Every scheduled run and published report must link its unit record and disclose the methodology version, research window, relevant relationship changes and publication date when one exists. Proposed or accepted inventory must not carry invented research or publication dates.

## Provider rights

A provider may:

- supply factual specifications and setup documentation;
- propose tests for consideration;
- report reproducibility problems;
- receive a short pre-publication window to identify factual errors;
- publish its own response after the report is public.

A provider may not:

- approve or suppress results;
- remove legitimate failures;
- demand positive language;
- obtain private participant or user data;
- claim an upstream merge before maintainers merge it;
- present sponsorship as certification or endorsement.
- require a particular operating system, test slot, publication date or amount of coverage.

## Intake and scheduling

Hardware acceptance means only that OHL has accepted custody under the published unit record. It does not guarantee testing, publication, a particular operating system or a positive result. OHL selects runs from available hardware, funds, research value, safety constraints and lab capacity.

Temporary loans require a workable research window and prepaid return path. Permanent placements transfer ownership to Arca Computer, Inc. before the unit is treated as a retained lab asset. Ambiguous “keep it if you like it” arrangements are not accepted.

## Corrections

Substantive corrections are logged in the report history. Quiet edits are reserved for spelling, formatting and clearly non-substantive mistakes.

## Conflicts

If a commercial relationship makes independent publication impossible, the lab will decline or terminate the intake and publish only the minimum disclosure legally and contractually permitted.
